# GY Keys – Complete Operating Instructions (Organic First)

**Last updated:** 26 October 2026

## 1. Principles
- Build slowly and carefully.
- For now, everything goes out organically.
- Free listings first to grow clientele and inventory.
- Join relevant Facebook groups and use Facebook Marketplace.
- Paid Boosts only if a client specifically asks for more views and approves the creative.
- Nothing paid goes live without client approval of the ad preview.
- Always declare Special Ad Category: Housing for any paid ads.
- Transparent reporting.

## 2. Free Listing Process
Use natural WhatsApp messages.

**Outreach / onboarding:**
"Hi [Name], I’m with GY Keys. We’re listing properties free right now. Send photos, location, price and a short description and I’ll get it live for you."

**After listing is live:**
"Your listing is up: [link]. People can message you directly on WhatsApp. I’ll also share it organically. Let me know if you want more exposure later."

## 3. Organic Distribution (Current Priority)
1. List the property free on the site.
2. Confirm photos and details with the client.
3. Post organically in relevant Facebook groups (Property For Sale or Rent Around Guyana, Guyana Real Estate groups, For Rent Guyana, local community groups in Georgetown / East Bank / East Coast).
4. Create a Facebook Marketplace listing (Property for Rent or Sale).
5. Share in WhatsApp status or broadcast where appropriate.
6. Do not spam. Follow each group’s rules.
7. Track where each listing is shared and any enquiries that come in.

**Sample organic post (groups & Marketplace):**
"New free listing on GY Keys: [beds] bed property in [area] for [price]. More photos and full details: [link]. Or message me on WhatsApp for viewings. Free to list your property too — just send the details."

## 4. When a Client Asks for More Views
Use natural language:
"Yeah no problem. Free listing is already live and I’ve shared it in groups and Marketplace. We can also run a 7-day Facebook Boost for G$8,900. We make a simple ad from your photos, run it locally, and send you a short report at the end. Want me to set it up?"

Only proceed if they confirm.

## 5. Paid Boost Process (Only on Request + Full Approval)
**Package:** 7-day Facebook Boost — G$8,900 (includes media spend, setup, creative, management, and report).

**Mandatory approval steps:**
1. Confirm payment.
2. Prepare ad creative from client photos (clear photo, price, location name, short text, listing link or WhatsApp).
3. Send preview to client and wait for written approval (WhatsApp).
4. Once approved, create the campaign.
5. Publish only after approval.
6. Send simple report at the end of 7 days.

**Ad setup steps:**
- Objective: Traffic or Leads
- Special Ad Category: Housing + country Guyana
- Location: Georgetown + 25–30 km radius (covers East Bank and East Coast). Use whole Guyana for land or higher-value properties.
- Advantage+ Audience and Advantage+ Placements: leave on if available.
- Budget: media portion over 7 days.
- Destination: listing page or Click-to-WhatsApp.

**Approval checklist before publishing:**
- Client approved the ad preview
- Payment confirmed
- Housing category selected
- Location correct
- Then go live.

## 6. Reporting
**Simple report template:**
Impressions: [number]
Clicks / Enquiries: [number]
Media spent: [amount]
Total fee: G$8,900
Notes: [any observations]

Send via WhatsApp.

## 7. Notes
- Always check Ads Manager for the exact options available under Housing.
- Start with one clean campaign per listing.
- Organic remains the priority until volume grows.
- Reply to enquiries quickly.
