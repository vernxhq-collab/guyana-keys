# GY Keys – Updated Operating Instructions (Organic First)

**Last updated:** 26 October 2026

## Principles
- For now, everything goes out organically.
- Free listings first to grow clientele and inventory.
- Join relevant Facebook groups and post to Facebook Marketplace.
- Paid Boosts only if the client specifically asks for more views AND approves the creative.
- Build slowly. Nothing paid goes live without client approval of the ad preview + payment confirmed.
- Always use Housing Special Ad Category for any paid ads.

## Free Listing Process
1. Collect photos, location, price, and short description via WhatsApp.
2. Get the free listing live on the site.
3. Confirm details with the client.
4. Share organically.

**Natural WhatsApp (welcome):**
"Hi [Name], your property is now live on GY Keys. Here's the link: [link]. People can message you directly on WhatsApp. I'll also share it in groups and on Marketplace. Let me know if you need changes."

## Organic Distribution (Current Priority)
1. List free on the site.
2. Join and post in relevant Facebook groups (search: Guyana properties, Georgetown real estate, East Bank rentals, local community groups).
3. Create Facebook Marketplace listing (Property for Sale/Rent).
4. Share link + WhatsApp contact.
5. Track enquiries. Do not spam.

**Sample group / Marketplace post:**
"Free listing now live on GY Keys. [Beds] bed in [area] for [price]. More photos and details: [link]. WhatsApp [number] for viewings. Also listing other properties free — message if you have one."

**Daily organic workflow:**
- New free listing live on site.
- Post once in 2–3 relevant groups.
- Create/update Marketplace listing.
- Reply to enquiries same day.

## When Client Asks for More Views
Natural reply:
"Yeah no problem. The free listing is already live and we're sharing it organically. If you want more exposure we can run a 7-day Facebook Boost for G$8,900. We create the ad from your photos, target local people, and send you a short report of impressions and enquiries. Want me to get it ready?"

## Paid Boost Process (Only After Approval)
1. Confirm payment (G$8,900).
2. Prepare creative (clear photo, price, location name, short text, WhatsApp or listing link).
3. Send ad preview to client and wait for approval.
4. Once approved, create campaign:
   - Objective: Traffic or Leads
   - Special Ad Category: Housing + Guyana
   - Location: Georgetown + 25–30 km (covers EBD/East Coast) or whole country for land/higher value
   - Advantage+ Audience and Placements on if available
   - Budget: media portion over 7 days
   - Destination: listing page or WhatsApp
5. Publish only after approval.
6. End of 7 days: short report.

**Approval Checklist (mandatory):**
- Client approved the ad preview
- Payment confirmed
- Housing category selected
- Location correct
→ Then go live.
