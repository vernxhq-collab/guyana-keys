# Inventory

30, 100, and 500+ live listings are goals. They are not a forecast and not a promise. A listing counts only when an approved agent publishes it, the owner has allowed it, and it meets the photo rule.

## Channels

- Facebook realtor outreach is already under way. The opener asks for the owner's permission before a home is listed. Do not copy a post, a photo, or a price onto Guyana Keys without that permission.
- Founding-agent offer. The first five approved accounts can carry the Founding agent badge. The free plan stays at 3 live listings. The offer is the badge and a direct review, not a fee waiver beyond the free plan.
- Owners selling or renting without an agent. They apply, or an agent they choose submits the home with the owner-permission box ticked.
- Developers. One contact per project. List only phases they confirm are for sale or rent.
- Owners outside Guyana. Same permission rule. The public page frames this as buying from outside Guyana: budget, location, an attorney, and a USD guide. Do not sort or score people by ethnicity or national origin.
- Referral loop. An approved agent can introduce another applicant. The new application still goes through the same checklist.

## Weekly goals

These are targets for the person doing outreach, not a commitment the site will hit them.

| Week | Goal |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 10 permission conversations opened. Applications recorded. No listing published without the checklist. |
| 2 | First live listings from approved accounts, toward 30. Each one has owner permission and at least 3 photos. |
| Ongoing | Hold the quality bar while moving toward 100, then 500+. Stop adding homes that miss a photo, a permission tick, or an approved account. |

## Quality bar

- The person listing it is an approved agent, or the application is still in review and the home stays hidden.
- Owner permission is ticked.
- At least 3 photos, confirmed as landscape, with no watermark and no other agency logo.
- Price is in GYD. The USD line is a guide at the rate already in the app. It is not a bank rate.
- Area is one of the existing areas. No invented neighbourhood stats.
- Title status is what the agent has on file. A listing is not proof of title.

## Permission

- Ask before using a photo, a price, or a description from Facebook, WhatsApp, or another site.
- Record the yes on the listing. If permission is refused or unclear, do not publish.
- Do not store ID images in the app. Record the check only. See `verification.md`.
- Do not present sample cards, invented agents, reviews, or testimonials as real.

## What to track

Count these from the database once the founding SQL has been applied. Until then, track them in the admin notes.

- Applications submitted, approved, and declined.
- Profiles approved, and how many have the founding badge.
- Live listings, hidden listings, and listings blocked for missing photos or permission.
- Enquiries per live listing.
- Listings removed because permission was withdrawn.

Do not publish a running total on the public site until the count is from live rows.
